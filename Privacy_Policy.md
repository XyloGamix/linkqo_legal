## 2. Information We Collect
Linqo is built with privacy in mind. We do NOT require user account creation, registration, passwords, or personal credentials such as your real name, home address, or phone number.

### A. Data Stored Locally on Your Device
To deliver a smooth offline experience, the following information is saved locally on your device:
- Game progression across levels (Levels 1 to 10,000)
- Star ratings, high scores, and remaining hints
- User preferences (Sound FX toggle, Haptic Vibration toggle, Visual Theme choice)

### B. Automatically Collected Technical & Telemetry Data
When your device connects to the internet, we and our service providers may process limited non-personal technical data:
- Anonymous Device Identifier: A randomized string (e.g. linqo_xxxxxxxx) generated locally to maintain leaderboard rankings without collecting personal identities.
- Anonymous Player Tag: A randomly generated nickname (e.g. Solver #1234) for leaderboards.
- Device & OS Specifications: Device manufacturer, model, Android OS version, screen resolution, and system language.
- Diagnostic Metrics: Game session frequency, playtime duration, level completion events, and crash logs to resolve bugs and improve game performance.
- IP Address: Standard network protocol transmission used for routing server requests and region-contextual advertising. We do not use IP addresses to track your physical identity.

---

## 3. Device Permissions Requested
Linqo requests only standard permissions essential to app functionality:
1. android.permission.INTERNET: Required to synchronize anonymous leaderboard scores with Supabase cloud servers, fetch game updates/notices, and load in-game advertisements from Google AdMob.
2. android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the app to verify active network connectivity before sending requests, conserving battery and data.

*Linqo DOES NOT access sensitive hardware features such as your Camera, Microphone, GPS Location, Contacts, SMS, Photos, or External Storage.*

## 5. How We Use Collected Information
We process data strictly for operational and gameplay purposes:
- Saving and restoring game progress and player scores.
- Displaying competitive global leaderboards.

## 6. Managing Personalized Ads (Opt-Out)
You can manage or opt out of personalized interest-based advertising on your Android device at any time:
- Android Settings: Open Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) and choose "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID".
- Web Opt-Out: Visit the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out portal at https://optout.aboutads.info/ or Network Advertising Initiative at https://optout.networkadvertising.org/.

## 8. Data Retention & Deletion Policy
- Local Progress Deletion: You can clear all local progress at any time via in-game Settings > "Reset All Progress", or by clearing app storage in Android Settings (Settings > Apps > Linqo > Storage > Clear Data).
- Cloud Data Deletion Request: If you wish to permanently purge any anonymous leaderboard or telemetry data associated with your device, email us at allgames9e@gmail.com with your anonymous Device ID (displayed in the in-game Settings dialog). We will complete your request within 30 days.
